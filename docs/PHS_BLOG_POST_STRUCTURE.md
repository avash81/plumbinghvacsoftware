# PHS Blog Post Structure

A single, consistent structure for every article. Simple to write, easy to read, and authoritative — no filler sections.

---

## Structure

**1. Title**
Plain language, includes the main keyword, under 60 characters.

**2. Intro**
2–3 sentences. State the problem and what the reader will learn. No backstory.

**3. Body**
- H2 for each main point, H3 only if a point needs sub-points
- Short paragraphs, 2–4 sentences
- Bullets or numbered lists over long prose
- One idea per section

**4. Visual (where it earns its place)**
A screenshot, table, or image only if it adds real understanding. Always include alt text.

**5. What to Do Next**
A short, direct action list — what the reader should actually do with this information.

**6. FAQ**
3–5 real questions people search, answered in 2–3 sentences each. Skip this on short articles where it's not needed.

**7. Byline**
Author name (linked to bio) and "Last updated: [date]."

---

## Template

```markdown
# [Title]

[2–3 sentence intro stating the problem and what will be learned.]

## [Main Point 1]

[Short paragraphs, bullets, or numbered lists.]

## [Main Point 2]

[Short paragraphs, bullets, or numbered lists.]

## What to Do Next

- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]
- [Action 3]

## Frequently Asked Questions

**[Question 1]**
[2–3 sentence answer.]

**[Question 2]**
[2–3 sentence answer.]

---
By [Author Name]
Last updated: [Date]
```
